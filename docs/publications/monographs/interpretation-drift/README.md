# Interpretation Drift

## Engineering Monograph Series

This publication documents the architectural rationale, empirical validation, and engineering principles that led to the interpretation drift mitigation architecture within the Behmanesh Structural Index (BSI).

Unlike traditional research papers, this monograph follows the evolution of an engineering problem from initial architectural observation through validation and implementation.

## Publication Structure

- Volume 1 — Interpretation Drift: Architectural Foundations
- Future volumes — Execution Engine, Framework Fidelity, Epistemic Integrity, and related architectural topics.
