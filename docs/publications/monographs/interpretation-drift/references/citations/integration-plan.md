# Citation Integration Plan

## Objective

Transform Volume 1 from an engineering document into a research-oriented monograph by integrating authoritative scientific references.

## Citation Categories

1. Foundations of Science
2. Systems Engineering
3. Software Architecture
4. Formal Verification
5. Artificial Intelligence
6. Large Language Models
7. Empirical Software Engineering

## Citation Rules

- Every major technical claim should be supported by at least one authoritative reference.
- Distinguish established knowledge from observations made within the BSI project.
- Clearly identify proposed architectural concepts introduced by this work.
- Prefer primary sources over secondary summaries.
