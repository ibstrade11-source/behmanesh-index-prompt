# Architecture Decision Records

## Purpose

This document records architectural decisions that govern the evolution of
Volume I.

Future implementation decisions shall remain consistent with these records.

---

## ADR-001

Decision:
Execution Governance is the architectural objective.

Rationale:
Interpretation Drift serves as observable evidence rather than the primary
subject.

---

## ADR-002

Decision:
Framework Fidelity is preserved rather than optimized.

Rationale:
Architectural stability precedes performance optimization.

---

## ADR-003

Decision:
Conceptual architecture precedes empirical validation.

Rationale:
Engineering systems require specification before experimentation.

---

## ADR-004

Decision:
Future volumes extend but do not redefine Volume I.

Rationale:
Longitudinal consistency is treated as an architectural requirement.
