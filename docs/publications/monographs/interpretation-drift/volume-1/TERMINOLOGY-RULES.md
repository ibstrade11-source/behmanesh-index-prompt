# Terminology Rules

Execution Governance:
Architectural objective.

Framework Fidelity:
Architectural property.

Interpretation Drift:
Observed architectural evidence.

EIG:
Architectural diagnostic mechanism.

These terms shall not be interchanged.

---

## Terminology Governance

Terminology shall evolve only by extension.

Existing architectural definitions shall never silently change meaning.

Whenever a new architectural concept is introduced it shall explicitly define:

- scope;
- relationship to existing concepts;
- architectural purpose;
- specification dependency.

Backward compatibility of terminology is considered mandatory.
