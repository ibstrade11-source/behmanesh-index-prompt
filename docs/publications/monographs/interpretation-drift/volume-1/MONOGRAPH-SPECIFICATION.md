# Monograph Specification

Architectural Objective:
Execution Governance.

Interpretation Drift is treated exclusively as architectural evidence
used to motivate the proposed execution-governance architecture.

No chapter should redefine Interpretation Drift as the primary objective
of the work.

---

## Architectural Constraints

The following constraints govern every revision of this volume.

- Architectural Objective shall remain invariant.
- Scope expansion shall not redefine architectural terminology.
- Future empirical evidence shall extend rather than replace the architectural
  specification.
- Subsequent implementation documents shall remain specification-compatible.
- Architectural continuity has priority over document growth.

---

## Specification Evolution Policy

Future editions may introduce additional architectural mechanisms.

However, every revision should preserve:

- architectural objective;
- architectural terminology;
- execution-governance perspective;
- declared scope;
- specification consistency.

Architectural maturity should increase while preserving foundational
specification.
