# Appendix F

# Future Research Agenda

## Purpose

Interpretation Drift represents the beginning of a broader engineering research program concerning reliable execution of formal analytical methodologies.

This appendix outlines several research directions extending beyond the scope of the present volume.

---

## Research Direction 1

Quantitative Framework Fidelity Metrics

Develop objective numerical measures capable of estimating execution fidelity independently of semantic quality.

---

## Research Direction 2

Automated Execution Verification

Investigate execution engines capable of continuously monitoring structural preservation during reasoning.

---

## Research Direction 3

Framework-Independent Validation

Evaluate whether interpretation drift exhibits similar characteristics across fundamentally different analytical methodologies.

---

## Research Direction 4

Cross-Generation LLM Studies

Determine whether future generations of language models naturally reduce interpretation drift or whether architectural support remains necessary.

---

## Research Direction 5

Formal Execution Languages

Investigate specification languages optimized for deterministic framework execution by probabilistic reasoning systems.

---

## Research Direction 6

Open Benchmark Development

Develop publicly reproducible benchmark suites dedicated to measuring framework fidelity and interpretation drift.

---

## Closing Remark

The present monograph documents the first stage of this engineering research program.

Future volumes are expected to expand both the theoretical foundations and the practical engineering mechanisms introduced here.
