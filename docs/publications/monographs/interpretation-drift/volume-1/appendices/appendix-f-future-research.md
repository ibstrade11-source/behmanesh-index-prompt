# Future Research

Future work should investigate execution-governance architectures across
additional analytical domains and independent language models.

Particular attention should be given to methods for evaluating
Framework Fidelity, specification preservation, execution traceability,
and reproducibility.

The objective of future research is to strengthen execution governance
rather than extend execution-governance architectures.

---

## Research Priorities

Future investigations should prioritize questions whose answers directly
improve architectural understanding.

Representative priorities include:

- specification evolution;
- governance scalability;
- execution reproducibility;
- automated consistency verification;
- formal specification languages;
- architectural interoperability.

---

## Expected Scientific Contribution

The long-term objective is the establishment of a reusable engineering
discipline for specification-governed analytical execution that remains
independent of individual language-model generations.
