# Design History

The interpretation drift architecture was not created in response to a single experimental outcome.

Instead, it evolved through repeated engineering observations made during the development of BSI.

Several architectural iterations gradually separated:

- specification,
- execution,
- validation,
- assessment.

Comparative experiments served primarily as validation of previously identified execution characteristics.

This distinction is important because it demonstrates that architectural evolution preceded empirical confirmation rather than resulting from it.

The design history therefore illustrates a typical engineering workflow:

Observation

↓

Hypothesis

↓

Validation

↓

Architecture

↓

Implementation
