## Citation Policy

References included within this bibliography should follow a consistent
scholarly citation format containing, whenever available:

- Author(s)
- Publication Year
- Title
- Venue or Publisher
- DOI or permanent identifier

Future revisions should progressively replace abbreviated entries with fully
specified bibliographic records.
