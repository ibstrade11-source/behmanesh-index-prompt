# Conclusion

This volume has presented the engineering rationale underlying the
Behmanesh Structural Index.

Rather than proposing modifications to Large Language Models, BSI
introduces an execution-governance architecture that structures the
execution of formally specified analytical procedures.

The observations documented throughout this volume provide engineering
evidence supporting the necessity of explicit execution governance for
professional LLM-assisted analytical workflows.

Future research should evaluate these architectural principles across
additional models, domains, and independent research groups.
