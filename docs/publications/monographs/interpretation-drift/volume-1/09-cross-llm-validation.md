# Cross-LLM Validation

## Objective

This chapter evaluates whether interpretation drift is observable across multiple Large Language Model (LLM) systems, rather than being specific to a single model implementation.

---

## Validation Approach

A set of identical analytical frameworks was executed across multiple LLM environments under controlled conditions.

The evaluation focused on:

- consistency of structural adherence,
- preservation of procedural constraints,
- and stability of execution across model boundaries.

---

## Observed Results

Across models, similar patterns were observed:

- partial adherence to framework structure,
- implicit modification of procedural steps,
- and preservation of semantic intent with structural deviation.

While the magnitude of drift varied, the structural pattern remained consistent.

---

## Interpretation

The consistency of observed behavior across models suggests that interpretation drift is not an isolated implementation artifact but a broader structural characteristic of LLM-based execution systems.

---

## Summary

Cross-model validation supports the hypothesis that interpretation drift is a systemic phenomenon in LLM execution environments.
