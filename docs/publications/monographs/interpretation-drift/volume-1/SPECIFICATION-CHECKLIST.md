# Specification Checklist

Before publication verify:

- Architectural Objective never changes.
- Evidence never becomes Objective.
- Terminology remains invariant.
- Chapter conclusions follow declared scope.
- No internal Interpretation Drift exists.

---

## Verification Questions

Before publication reviewers should additionally verify:

- Does every chapter support the declared objective?
- Does every conclusion remain inside declared scope?
- Has any architectural definition changed?
- Have new sections introduced specification drift?
- Are appendices consistent with the architectural baseline?
