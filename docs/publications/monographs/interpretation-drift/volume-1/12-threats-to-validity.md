# Chapter 12
# Threats to Validity

The purpose of this study is not to evaluate or rank Large Language
Models.

Nor does it seek to explain their internal implementation.

The architectural conclusions presented throughout this volume are
limited to the execution-governance mechanisms proposed by the
Behmanesh Structural Index.

Observed execution behaviors are interpreted as engineering evidence
supporting architectural decisions.

Further validation across additional analytical domains, execution
frameworks, and independent research groups remains future work.

---

## Research Scope Limitations

This volume intentionally prioritizes conceptual architecture over empirical
verification.

Accordingly, architectural validity should not be interpreted as empirical
effectiveness.

Empirical validation is deferred to subsequent volumes.

---

## Author Perspective

The proposed architecture has been developed by the same research program
that evaluates it.

Although architectural specifications, audit procedures, and consistency
checks reduce internal inconsistency, future independent replication remains
necessary.

---

## Framework Generalizability

Current observations originate primarily from analyses involving the
Behmanesh Structural Index.

Future research should evaluate the architecture using multiple independent
analytical frameworks to determine broader applicability.
