# Chapter 7
# Architectural Interpretation

## Scope

Root cause analysis is performed at the execution architecture level.

No claim is made regarding the internal implementation of language
models.

## Engineering Interpretation

Observed deviations indicate the necessity of explicit execution
governance.

Accordingly, architectural control is introduced outside the language
model through the Behmanesh Structural Index.

Interpretation Drift therefore represents architectural evidence rather
than the architectural objective itself.

---

## Architectural Interpretation Process

Architectural interpretation is performed by separating observable execution
behavior from architectural intent.

This separation prevents isolated execution failures from being interpreted
as architectural failures.

Observed execution behavior shall therefore be interpreted through the
following sequence:

1. Specification
2. Execution
3. Observation
4. Structural interpretation
5. Architectural conclusion

Only after this sequence has been completed may architectural conclusions be
drawn.

---

## Interpretation Constraints

Architectural interpretation shall never infer architectural properties from
a single execution trace.

Multiple independent observations are required before identifying recurring
structural behavior.

Consequently, isolated execution anomalies should be interpreted as
observations requiring further investigation rather than immediate evidence
for architectural modification.
