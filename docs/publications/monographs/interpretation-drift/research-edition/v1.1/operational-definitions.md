# Operational Definitions

## Interpretation Drift

Observable divergence between a formally specified analytical procedure and its executed realization.

## Framework Fidelity

Degree of structural preservation of a predefined analytical framework during execution.

## Structural Consistency

Stability of procedural organization across repeated executions.

## Execution Traceability

Ability to map each analytical step back to an explicit specification element.

## Decision Traceability

Ability to justify every analytical conclusion using identifiable procedural evidence.
