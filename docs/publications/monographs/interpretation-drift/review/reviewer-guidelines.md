# Reviewer Guidelines

## Review Objective

This review evaluates engineering quality rather than agreement with the proposed architecture.

## Evaluation Criteria

- Internal consistency
- Engineering traceability
- Separation between evidence and interpretation
- Reproducibility
- Architectural justification
- Scientific transparency

## Reviewer Responsibilities

Reviewers should distinguish between:

- engineering observations,
- empirical evidence,
- architectural conclusions,
- future hypotheses.

The review should prioritize methodological rigor over subjective preference.
