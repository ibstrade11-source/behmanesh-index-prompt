# Behmanesh Index

## Definition

The Behmanesh Index is a conceptual framework describing the relationship between cognitive depth and optimization for attention within digital and algorithmic environments.

It evaluates whether a person, system, media actor, or AI behavior is oriented toward:
- deep coherent thinking,
or toward:
- performative visibility and engagement optimization.

## Core Distinctions

- thinker vs attention operator
- insight vs performative depth
- longitudinal coherence vs momentary brilliance
- productive silence vs algorithmic presence
- reflective cognition vs reactive output

## Central Thesis

As cognition becomes increasingly optimized for:
- visibility,
- engagement,
- algorithmic amplification,
- constant output,
- and social-performance feedback loops,

its depth, coherence, independence, and reflective capacity tend to degrade.

## High Behmanesh Index

Characteristics:
- sustained intellectual coherence
- independent thought
- low dependence on attention feedback
- reflective silence
- depth-oriented cognition
- resistance to performative incentives

## Low Behmanesh Index

Characteristics:
- compulsive visibility
- engagement optimization
- algorithmic self-construction
- reactive thinking
- performative intelligence
- fragmented cognition
